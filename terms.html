What is free

The app is free to download. You can scan unlimited pages, crop, apply filters, and export a single page as PDF or JPG with no watermark. The free limit is shown in the app before you scan and again if a paid action is blocked. If a multi-page export is blocked, you can still export the first page for free.

Paid plans

  • Yearly — US$19.99 per year
  • Lifetime — US$49.99, one payment

Both options are shown together on the purchase screen, each with its price, before you are asked to pay. Prices shown are for the United States App Store and may differ elsewhere. There is no free trial that converts into a charge.

A paid plan unlocks merging pages into one PDF, on-device OCR (recognize and copy), batch export, and high-resolution JPG export.

Lifetime covers every scanning feature in the app at the time you buy it and every future feature we add to Neatscan. Features that shipped with the lifetime plan are not later moved behind the yearly plan only.

Yearly renewal

Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. The yearly plan renews automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel the yearly plan in your Apple Account settings after purchase.

If a yearly plan ends

Paid actions become unavailable, but the documents you already saved remain on the device and stay readable. You can still export the first page of a document. Your scans are not held hostage.

Your content

You keep the rights to the paper you scan and the files you export. Neatscan does not claim ownership of your documents. You are responsible for what you scan and for how you share the files you create.
